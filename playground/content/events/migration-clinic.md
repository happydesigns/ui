This fictional session demonstrates how event content appears in lists, search, and detail routes.

## Topics

- Inventory local and reusable behavior
- Establish a prerelease gate
- Preserve routes, content, and media
- Compare responsive screenshots before and after migration

## Expected outcome

A migration sequence that keeps every step reviewable and leaves project-specific composition in the consumer.
## Working format

The example uses a 90-minute remote clinic: 20 minutes for inventory, 40 minutes for a scoped migration slice, and 30 minutes for generated-output and responsive checks.

Participants would bring one existing route, its local implementation, and a short list of visible behaviors that must remain unchanged.
