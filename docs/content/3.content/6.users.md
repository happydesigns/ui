---
title: "Users"
description: "Use the user collection for authors and people referenced from content."
---
Use the `user` schema for people referenced from content. The built-in article schema includes author references, and article lists resolve them against the configured user collection. Other collections can add their own references.

## Use users for

- Article authors.
- Event contacts.
- People displayed by reusable trait components.

## Keep local when

A person only appears once in prose and does not need structured rendering, link metadata, or reuse across content. In that case, normal Markdown text is enough.
