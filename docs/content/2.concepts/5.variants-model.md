The Nuxt Content layer uses variants to compose collection features and presentation defaults. An article can have dates, authors, categories, copy buttons, and surrounding links. An event shares several of those features and adds location and external links.

Extending `@happydesigns/ui/content` registers the collection variants and loads their defaults. Apps can override those defaults in `app.config.ts`. Base-layer article components take typed data and use their own [layout settings](/components/article/portable-article-layouts#defaults-and-overrides).

## How to use variants

Use a variant when a feature is part of a repeatable content type. For example, article-like collections can share category badges and list settings, while a one-off project page can stay local.

Do not use variants to hide product behavior or authorization rules. Variants are a presentation and content-structure tool. Domain rules belong in the product or app that owns the behavior.
